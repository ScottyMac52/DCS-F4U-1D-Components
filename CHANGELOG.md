# Changelog

### Added

- Dual GUID-qualified Logitech Flight Throttle Quadrant profiles from Scott's current
  F4U-1D export.

  controls plus the secondary supercharger axis.
- Photo-backed dual-quadrant OpenKneeboard reference and device documentation.
- Automated quadrant identity, axis-ownership, button, unused-control, alias-isolation,
  documentation, and package validation.

  Scott's current DCS F4U-1D export.
- Native flaps, approach-light, airbrake, wing-fold, wing-lock, hook, landing-gear,
  parking-brake, stores-selector, drop-tank-lock, and emergency-release mappings.
- Two photo-backed OpenKneeboard pages and complete device documentation.
- Deterministic OvGME packaging and automated validation.
